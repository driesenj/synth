## Notes

- R80–R85 exist on both boards: on the main board they are the DAC output stages' resistors, on the faceplate the 470 Ω LED resistors. The Board column says which.
- 33 of the main board's resistors stand up on a 2.54 mm footprint: any 1/4 W or LR1F part fits.
- D21, the LM4040-2.5 in TO-92: its cathode is the middle pin; the board ties pads 1 and 3 to the anode.
- If the TO-92 LM4040 can't be had, the SOT-23 one (LM4040BIM3-2.5/NOPB, Conrad) fits D21 on two short wire legs: its pin 1 (cathode) into the middle hole, pin 2 (anode) into either outer hole, pin 3 left free. Pins 1 and 2 are the two leads on one side, pin 1 on the left with the marking upright.
- EasyEDA's own BOM of the routed project calls H3, H6, HF3 and HF6 an LCSC female 1×3: fit male pins on the faceplate side.
- Fit R33, R36 and R39 (flat, under the breakout) and the two centre mounting screws before plugging the breakout in.
- Solder the faceplate's four DIP-16 chips (U1, U9, U10, U11) straight to the board, without sockets: in a socket they reach to within 0.9–1.5 mm of the main board, where the breakout's socket-strip tails stick down about 1.4 mm, and U11's socket would touch the standoff beside it. Mind pin 1 before soldering.
